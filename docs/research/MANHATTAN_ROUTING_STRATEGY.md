## Provider-neutral contract

`RouteGraph` contains WGS84 `RouteNode` and directed `RouteEdge` records. Nodes retain source IDs and feature IDs so any selected building, POI, area, station, or entrance can become an endpoint. Edges retain modes (`walking` or `transit`), distance/duration provenance, source references, freshness, accessibility (`yes`/`no`/`unknown`), and geometry semantics (`pedestrian-centerline` or `transit-schematic`). `Itinerary`, `ItineraryLeg`, and `RouteStep` retain the same source/license/uncertainty chain.

The local adapter is intentionally deterministic: SHA-256 and immutable metadata are required; local paths only; pending sources fail before output; duplicate IDs, missing topology endpoints, invalid geometry, unsupported modes, invalid distances/durations, and records outside the Manhattan slice are rejected and counted. Dijkstra chooses lowest total synthetic distance, then duration, then lexical edge-path order. Snapping is exact by feature ID where possible and otherwise reports distance and uncertainty; it is not a sidewalk guarantee.

## Product safety and approval questions

The UI's Directions panel, route overlay, step list, and camera journey are explicitly synthetic fixture previews. They do not represent live navigation, current transit, exact travel time, step-free routing, sidewalk accessibility, or Street View. Reduced-motion preference shortens camera flights, and Stop/Clear/unmount cancel flights and timers.

Before any real graph is acquired or enabled, approve: (1) one specific Overture release or OSM extract and its license/attribution/derivative treatment; (2) NYC DCP/DOT contextual products and their per-dataset terms; (3) MTA static feed/transfers and station joins; (4) any live operational source, API key, rate limit, retention, and privacy policy; and (5) whether route geometry may be redistributed as derived tiles/indexes.

## Exact first post-approval command

After written approval for one source and its terms, place the approved immutable local graph at `data/approved/manhattan-route-graph.json`, update that source registry entry from `pending` to `approved`, record its checksum/release metadata, then run:

```sh
pnpm route:ingest -- --input data/approved/manhattan-route-graph.json --output data/normalized/manhattan-route-graph-<sha256-prefix> --checksum <64-hex-sha256> --ingested-at 2026-08-03T00:00:00Z
```

The command refuses URLs, pending entries, bad checksums, malformed topology, and existing output directories; it does not contact a provider.
